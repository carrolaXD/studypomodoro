Support

Use Study Pomodoro on a PC with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Install it from the Microsoft Store.

Do I need an account?

You can use the timer, sounds, and statistics offline. Google sign-in is optional and syncs your progress and settings across PCs.

How can I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Open the feedback form. For a bug report, include your app version, Windows version, and the steps to reproduce the problem.

How do I delete synced data?

Email pedrocarrola2006@gmail.com to request deletion. The privacy policy explains what data is stored and where.
