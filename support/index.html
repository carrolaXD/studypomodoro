Support

Install on Windows

On a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, open the Microsoft Store listing and select Get.

Keep the timer above your work

Switch to Mini Mode in the app. The compact window stays above your notes and lets you pause or resume the countdown.

Sync or delete your data

Sign in with Google in settings to sync progress across PCs. For deletion requests, use the contact in the privacy policy.

Contact the developer

Send feedback with your app version and the steps that led to the issue.
