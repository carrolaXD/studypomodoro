Support

Installation, timer controls and help with your data.

How do I install the app?
Open the Microsoft Store on a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC. Get it from Microsoft Store ↗
How does Mini Mode work?
Switch to Mini Mode in the app to keep a small timer above other windows. You can still pause and resume the session.
Can I study offline?
Yes. Timer, sounds and local statistics work offline. Google sign-in is optional for syncing progress and preferences across PCs.
How do I delete my synced data?
Use the contact in the privacy policy.
